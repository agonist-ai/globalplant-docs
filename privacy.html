GlobalPlant — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Publisher: Agonist Development AB, Sweden

Summary

GlobalPlant keeps your garden on your device. The app has no account, does not collect personal data, does not track you, and contains no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your plants, animals, diary entries, photos, care reminders, notes, harvest records and settings are stored only on your iPhone (in the app's private storage and the app group shared with the GlobalPlant home-screen widget). We do not receive this data and cannot see it.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a plant for your diary or diagnosis. Photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded. The app asks for permission to add a harvest card to your photo library only when you save one.

Notifications

Care and harvest reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are optional and you can turn them off in iOS Settings.

Sharing and export

Sharing a harvest card or exporting your data happens only when you start it, through the iOS share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is your choice.

Community

In this version the Community tab works with content stored on your device only; nothing you write there is sent to other people or to our servers.

Advice is informational

Plant advice and diagnoses are informational suggestions, not professional agronomic, veterinary or medical advice. Check withdrawal periods and local rules before acting on them.

Children

GlobalPlant is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Third parties and tracking

This version sends no data to third parties and uses no analytics, advertising or cross-app tracking. If a future version adds any online feature, this policy will be updated first and the App Store privacy information will change with it.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side; deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. You may contact us with any privacy question.
